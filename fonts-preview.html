Sans-serif options.

All five are in heavy use across the web, so they read as familiar rather than as a choice. Body text stays Assistant throughout — only the headings, prices and the wordmark change.

One trade-off worth knowing. The brand deck paired a serif display with a sans body, and that contrast is doing some work. All-sans is cleaner and more familiar, but it is also what most gym websites look like. Option 1 keeps things tightest by using the font already in the brand.
