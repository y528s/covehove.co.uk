The S and the J.

The current typeface is Fraunces, chosen in the brand deck. It has a wonk setting that deliberately skews certain letters, and turning it off (option 2) gives the J a normal descending hook. It does not change the S noticeably — so if the S is the problem, the answer is a different typeface, options 3 to 5.

Each option below shows the headline, the price figure, and the two letters in question at large size. Body text stays Assistant either way.
