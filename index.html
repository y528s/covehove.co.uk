Welcome

A place to move, not a place to prove anything.

We built Cove for people who have never called themselves gym people. People coming back to movement after a long gap. People who want to keep doing the things they love for years yet. People who just want somewhere close to home to feel a bit better in.

Every membership starts with a 1-to-1 induction with one of our trainers. You’ll be shown around, talked through the kit, and between you you’ll work out what a good week actually looks like for you. Nobody is left to figure it out on their own.

Come for a class, stay for a coffee. This is your village, and we’d like you to feel at home in it.

Questions

The things people ask us.

I haven’t exercised in years. Is this for me?

Yes. That is exactly who we built it for. Every membership starts with a 1-to-1 induction, so you will never be left standing in the middle of the room wondering what to do next.

Is there a contract?

No. Membership is monthly and you can cancel any time — just let us know.

What is the joining fee for?

Your 1-to-1 induction and getting you set up as a member. It is a one-off £30, paid when you join.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes you can move in, trainers for the gym floor, and a water bottle. Mats and props are already in the studio, so there is nothing to carry for a class.

Can I look around first?

Please do. Book a free tour using the form on this page and we will show you both spaces, answer your questions and leave you to think about it.
